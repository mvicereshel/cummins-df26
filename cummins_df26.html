Pro Tips for Success

Arrive Early

  • Avoid flying in the morning of Day 1 to maximize your time on the campus
  • Walk the grounds to understand where things are
  • Take a look at the campus map so you have an understanding of where booths/keynotes etc are
  • Better direct customers based on your knowledge of the layout

During the Event

  • Wear comfortable shoes
  • Bring a reusable water bottle & have a food plan
  • Bring mobile charging blocks
  • Use Slack channels, WhatsApp, or text groups with customers for on-site reminders and support
  • Consider time differences when scheduling meetings
  • Attend Dreamfest with customers
  • Build in time for breaks and walking around

Bookmark Sessions on Salesforce+

  • Can't squeeze in all the sessions? No problem!
  • Bookmark the sessions that are going to be available on Salesforce+
  • Catch them after the event at your own pace

Install the App Before You Go

  • Everything you need for Dreamforce, all in one app
  • Check your schedule on the go
  • Submit feedback on sessions in real-time
  • Get personalized recommendations based on your interests
  • Keep important event updates and useful resources at your fingertips

Safety & Security Best Practices

Travel
  • Take cash and valuables with you; snap a pic of hotel safe contents
  • Keep room key separate from sleeve with your room number
  • In restaurants, keep your bag in your lap or between your legs
  • Walk with a buddy especially after dark
  • Stay alert in crowded, high-traffic areas
Credentials
  • Remove credentials once you exit the Dreamforce campus
  • Sharing your credentials is strictly prohibited
  • Don't dispose of credentials in public trash; use on-site recycling
  • Don't post pictures of credentials on social media
